Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach the developer.

Add the lock screen widget

  1. Open the app once so it can download the timetable.
  2. Lock your iPhone, then press and hold the lock screen and tap Customize.
  3. Tap Lock Screen, then tap the widget area under the clock.
  4. Find the app in the list and tap its widget to add it. Tap Done.

To change how it looks, open the Widget tab in the app, pick a preset or adjust the options, then tap Save to widget.

Change your station

  1. Tap the round station button to the left of the tab bar.
  2. Pick a station under "Always from" to keep that station, or pick the row under "Nearest to you" to follow your location.

Keep the widget on your nearest station

With "While Using" location access, the station updates when the app or the widget refreshes. With "Always", it also updates in the background as you reach a station.

  1. Open the iPhone's Settings app, then Apps, then find the app.
  2. Tap Location and choose Always.

You can also do this from the app: Widget tab, the menu button at the top right, Station, "Allow in background (Always)".

Report a wrong time

  1. In the Trains tab, press and hold the train that looks wrong.
  2. Tap "Report a schedule issue". An email opens, already filled in with the train and timetable.
  3. Add what was wrong and send it.

Times look out of date

The timetable is checked about once a day. To check now, open the Widget tab, tap the menu button at the top right, open Schedule and tap "Update schedule now". Live times need a connection, so underground the app shows scheduled times.
