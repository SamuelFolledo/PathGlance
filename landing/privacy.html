Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

The app collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your location is used on your phone only and is never sent anywhere.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location to pick the nearest PATH station for the app and the lock screen widget. This happens on your phone. Your location is not sent to the developer or to anyone else, and it is not stored on any server.

Location access is optional. Without it, you pick a station yourself. You can change the permission at any time in the iPhone's Settings app.

What is stored

Your settings (station, favorite stops, widget look, muted issues, text size) and the downloaded timetable are stored on your phone only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Network requests

The app downloads timetables, live arrival times and service alerts from the Port Authority's public website, panynj.gov. These are read-only requests that carry no information about you or your location. As with any website visit, the Port Authority's servers see your device's IP address. The app contacts no other servers.

Email

Contacting the developer or reporting a wrong time opens your own mail app with a draft. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and the developer receives only what is in that email.

TestFlight

During the beta the app is distributed through Apple's TestFlight. Crash reports and feedback you choose to submit there are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy and shared with the developer.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
