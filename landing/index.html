A look inside

What it does

🔒

Lock screen widget

The next departures toward each terminal, right under the clock. Pick a ready-made look or set the font, size and time format yourself.

📍

Follows your station

The app and the widget start from the PATH station nearest you. Or pick one station and keep it.

⭐️

Favorite stops

Add the stops you care about as columns and see when each train reaches them, side by side.

🚉

Every stop of every train

Tap a train to see each stop and its time. Step to the train before or after without closing the sheet.

⏱️

Live times and late marks

When PATH reports a live arrival, the app shows it and marks trains that are running late.

⚠️

Issues you can mute

Service alerts get their own tab. Mute the kinds you don't need, such as elevator notices.

📶

Works underground

The timetable is saved on your phone, so times still show with no signal in the tunnel.

🙈

No account, no tracking

Nothing to sign up for. Your location stays on your phone, and there are no ads or analytics.

Set up in 3 steps

  1. 1
    Open the app

    Allow location and it finds your nearest station. Skip that and pick a station yourself.

  2. 2
    Add the widget

    Press and hold your lock screen, tap Customize, then add the widget under the clock.

  3. 3
    Make it yours

    Choose a look in the Widget tab and tap Save to widget. Your lock screen updates to match.

Requirements

  • iPhone with iOS 26 or later
  • Apple's free TestFlight app, during the beta
  • Location access is optional
